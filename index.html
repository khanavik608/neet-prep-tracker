        Study Streak & Highest Session Rules
        1. Day Streak Rules:

        You must log at least 10 minutes (600 seconds) of study time within a calendar day (IST timezone) to maintain your daily streak. If an entire calendar day passes with 0 study time recorded, your streak will reset to 0.

        2. Highest Session Record Rules:

        Cumulative daily hours do not trigger celebrations. A celebration pop-up appears strictly when you complete a single continuous study session (via Stopwatch save or Pomodoro completion) that breaks your previous longest single-session record.

        3. Reset Option:

        You can reset your Highest Session record anytime by clicking the trash icon next to your focus metrics badge if you want a fresh start.
